PRODUCT, DESIGN, AND DEVELOP

I’m a Data Science and Economics student at UC Berkeley, passionate about shaping technology into products people actually use and trust. I enjoy breaking down complex ideas, understanding user needs, and combining data, human-centered design, and collaboration to create intuitive, end-to-end experiences.

Beyond the final product, I’m drawn to the process of asking better questions, challenging assumptions, and refining ideas through iteration. I believe the best products come from understanding people, making thoughtful decisions, and continuously learning along the way.
